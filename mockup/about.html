A little about how I work

The unfamiliar is a place to begin.

I like finding the shape of a system by getting close to it: tracing how it behaves, trying a model, and learning where that model breaks.

Technical intuition

It comes from repetition. Build something, discover what you misunderstood, adjust, and try again. Over time, the question changes from “what tool should I use?” to “what is this system asking me to notice?”

Adaptability, pattern recognition, and judgment: qualities made practical through implementation and experiments.

How I build

My strongest interests sit around implementation, experiments, and architecture. I enjoy getting a first working model into the world, then examining its limits with care.

I value clear decisions, honest evidence, and taking responsibility for what happens after the first attempt.

Now

I’m a founder at Cerenetics, working across software, AI, research, and technical experiments.

This site is about my own engineering path. Company projects will be labeled as company work, with my personal contribution made clear.
