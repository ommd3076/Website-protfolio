A WORKING NOTE

Sometimes a problem gives us a tool that outlives it.

Newton’s questions about motion helped drive his work on fluxions, one of the developments that formed calculus. Leibniz developed calculus independently and introduced notation that became widely used. A need in one field helped push another toward a new instrument for describing change.

I keep thinking about that kind of exchange. Better tools can extend what people can do. A more precise instrument can give a surgeon greater control; careful tools for research can help make new questions tractable. Each gain can make the next one possible.

That is the engineering I care about: building the right instrument for a real task, with enough context and care that it helps someone do meaningful work better.
